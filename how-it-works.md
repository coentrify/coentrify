# How It Works

How the system turns any input into what you want.

## The Quality Factory

Coentrify is a funnel and a quality transformer.

- The funnel gathers every input, in any quality and any language.
- The system turns the input into layers: docs, specs and tests.
- The way of working turns the layers into output.

Together with the pillars, the funnel and the transformer form a quality factory. What comes out is what you want.

## The Quality Of The Question

The quality of an answer depends on the quality of the question.

Coentrify is a collection of carefully curated questions and statements. The collection works as a pre-prompt: an agent reads the collection before the prompt, so a simple prompt still gets a quality answer.

A one-word prompt works for the same reason. The questions were already asked, in writing.

## The Compass

A good system extracts only what the system needs from the director. The vision, the pillars and the laws are the compass. With a direction and a way to steer around obstacles, nobody has to provide the complete route.
