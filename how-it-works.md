# How It Works

How the system turns any input into what you want.

## The Quality Factory

Coentrify is a funnel and a quality transformer.

- The funnel gathers every input, in any quality and any language.
- The system turns the input into layers: docs, specs and tests.
- The work turns the layers into output.

Together with the pillars, the funnel and the transformer form a quality factory. What comes out is what you want.

## The Quality Of The Question

The quality of an answer depends on the quality of the question.

Coentrify is a collection of carefully curated questions and statements. The collection works as a pre-prompt: an agent reads the collection before the prompt, so a simple prompt still gets a quality answer.

A one-word prompt works for the same reason. The questions were already asked, in writing.

## The Compass

A good system extracts only what the system needs from the director. The vision, the pillars and the laws are the compass. With a direction and a way to steer around obstacles, nobody has to provide the complete route.

## The Loop

A gap or a mistake goes back into the funnel as input. The fix becomes a standard or a check, so the factory gets better with every round ([Standards](standards.md)).

## The Work

The director decides what gets built. The agent works from the specs, one use case at a time. A test is written first, and fails. The agent builds until the test passes and every check runs clean.

The agent settles the easy choices and lists them for the director to veto. The director looks at the proof and decides whether the work is done.

## The Director

The director is the human who directs the project. The director has three responsibilities in the work, and three around the work.

### In The Work

- Specify - explain, refine and answer questions
- Plan - choose what comes next and what comes later
- Accept - check the proof

### Around The Work

- Steer - set the vision, and direct how the system heals and personalizes itself
- Own - answer for the result and for everything that touches the outside world
- Provide - give the agents access, tools and a budget
