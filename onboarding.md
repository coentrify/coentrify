## Choices

A project adopts the parts it wants. A project can skip a part and still adopt the others, such as Make No Mistakes.

### Workflow

These are possible workflows, and more can follow.

- None - the project keeps its own way of working
- Simple - flat lists: an inbox, a plan, a backlog and a roadmap
- Tickets - every piece of work is a ticket, from intake to verdict

### Records

- A request log and an activity log
- No logs
