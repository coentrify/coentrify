## The Direction

- One dial runs from the director as full creative director, as today, to the agent as director with the director as a stakeholder.
- At the agent's end, the director's input is the brief, in any shape and any length: a jam theme, a pitch with tone and pillars, a list of elements, a book.
- The autonomous end is opt-in: a setting in `coentrify.json`, and most likely a module of its own. Games are its first pilot, and it can be generalized later.
- The standards are the default. Flavors and overrides customize a game.
- No default taste. The output shares only positive qualities, such as polished, well designed and fun, never a surface trait such as blue buttons on every screen, so no game looks like a coentrify game.
- Flexible, and strongly convinced of the practices proven to work.
- Pilots and playtests are essential. A pilot measures fun, coherence, vibe and how well the choices and assets work together, beside the share the director vetoed.
- Each piece is filed one by one, and settled with the director one by one.
