## How A Layer Is Brought

Each layer comes to the director one at a time, with its title, description, flavors, examples, considerations, famous sources and important notes, then the recommendation and the question for the go.

## The Sweep

- Sources are design writing, talks, postmortems and public games, each named. Public games stay references, never examples on the page.
- Examples are made up, recognizable with tension, and never from the director's own games.
- A flavor is taste, with a `Weighs:` line for what it gives and what it costs. A pattern is a device proven to work.
- A layer's flavors are its main ones, never all there are, and they mix by scope where they can, each pick naming what it covers after a dash.
- A flavor that sets a law aside says so, and a game that picks it leaves the law out with its reason. A flavor never comes in over a law quietly.
- The layers so far, in the index's order: Tone, Kind Of Play, Story Delivery, Protagonist, Story Structure, Plot, Choice, Morality, Opening, Ending, Era, The Secret, Reveal, Conventions, Death, Crafting, Mistakes, Maker Presence, Naming.

## The Candidates

- World Structure: Linear, Hub And Spokes, Interconnected, Open, Procedural. Ties to No Artificial Gates and Wayfinding.
- Power: Skill Only, Stats, Gear, Meta Progression. Ties to Earned Progress.
- Difficulty: Fixed, Chosen, Adaptive after Hunicke 2005, Assist Options. Ties to Bands, Not Bullseyes and No Try Hard.
- Time: Real Time, Turn Based, Pausable, The Clock.
- Art Style: Pixel Art, Low Poly, Hand Drawn, Cel Shaded, Realistic, Voxel, for Asset Layers' style.
- Music: Adaptive Score, Diegetic Only, Leitmotifs, Sparse, after Collins, Game Sound, 2008.
- Together: Solo, Co-op, Competitive, Asynchronous Traces.
- The Fall: the hero loses everything mid-game and rebuilds, Booker's central crisis of Rags To Riches, a sibling of the No-Gear Level.
- Kishōtenketsu Level: introduce, develop, twist, conclude, one idea per level, after Hayashida, 2012.
- Juice: exaggerated feedback on every action, after Jonasson and Purho, 2012.
- Coyote Time And Input Buffer: forgiving input windows, tied to Bands, Not Bullseyes.
- Pity Timer: bad luck protection, within the Values laws.
- Chekhov's Gun: what is shown early pays off later.
- Breadcrumbs: small rewards along the intended path, tied to Wayfinding.
- The Body Tells The Story: the character's body grows with strength and deeds.
