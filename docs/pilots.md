# Pilots

A pilot makes a small game end to end, to show where the system feels off. Pilots run on `autonomous` and on `guided`, from 0.3.0 on.

- The Run - how a pilot is made
- The Playtest - how it is judged
- The Template - the page of one pilot
- After - where the gaps go

## The Run

- A fresh machine, a fresh folder, and coentrify at its released version.
- `coentrify game`, the mode, and one input: a jam theme or a short pitch.
- The scope is a game jam entry.
- At `guided`, the director answers the questions, and notes where a question was missing, odd or one too many.
- The run ends at a playable build.

## The Playtest

- People who did not make the game play it.
- Each one writes what felt off and what worked.
- Each one rates fun, coherence, vibe and synergy from 1 to 5, as a feeling, with a line why.

## The Template

```
# Pilot <date>

- Mode: autonomous or guided
- Input: the prompt, word for word
- Models: the model of each session
- Version: the coentrify version

## The Run

- Where it stopped, stalled or asked.
- The vetoed share per phase: the vetoed choices out of all choices in each phase's veto list.

## The Playtest

- Per player: fun, coherence, vibe and synergy, 1 to 5 each, with a line why.
- What felt off.
- What worked.

## What Heals

- Each gap, and the change it asks for: a flavor, a check question, a template or a rule.
```

## After

- Each gap is filed with coentrify, and goes through [The Conduct](contributions.md#the-conduct).
- What worked is named too, so no change breaks it.
- The results join the [Proof](../proof.md) once a few pilots can be compared.
