# Laws And Psychology

The worklist for the game laws checked against what is known about people. The findings, with their checked sources, are in the [Psychology](../standards/psychology/overview.md) module. What is left is what the check found: gaps no law covers, and findings no law rests on. An item leaves the list once the director has ruled on it.

- The Gaps - what people need that no law covers yet
- Findings With No Law - a finding that no law rests on yet

## The Gaps

- Cadence - the rhythm and pace of play and story: how intensity rises and falls ([The Peak-End Rule](../standards/psychology/the-peak-end-rule.md)).
- Curiosity, never compulsion - nothing draws the line between rewarding curiosity and building a compulsion loop ([Variable Reward Schedules](../standards/psychology/variable-reward-schedules.md)).
- Color alone - nothing says state never rests on color alone ([Color Vision Deficiency](../standards/psychology/color-vision-deficiency.md)).

## Findings With No Law

- [Color Vision Deficiency](../standards/psychology/color-vision-deficiency.md) - its law comes from the gap Color alone.
