# Laws And Psychology

The worklist for the game laws checked against what is known about people. The findings, with their checked sources, are in the [Psychology](../standards/psychology/overview.md) module. What is left is what the check found: weak points in the laws, and gaps no law covers. An item leaves the list once the director has ruled on it.

- The Weak Points - a law that claims more than its finding gives, or rests on no finding
- The Gaps - what people need that no law covers yet

## The Weak Points

- Contrast - a drive out feels like an escape. Rest comes from soft fascination, never from emptiness, which fits the sparse area with its one find. Optimal arousal supports Contrast only weakly.
- Fair Surprise - the player reads a risk and partly steers it. Controllability backs the steering. Reading a risk in advance is predictability, which it does not cover.
- Bands, Not Bullseyes - it rests on flow. In the one controlled game study, the balance of challenge and skill alone changed nothing, so the band rests more on competence and control.
- Worth Reading - a missed line stays missed. Scarcity was measured with objects, and a line lost for good frustrates a slow reader.
- Soft Sound - a repeated sound varies. The variation mainly keeps a repeat from sounding mechanical, spacing lets a sound recover, and a cue the player must notice stays recognizable.
- No Hard Edges - the preference for curves is moderate, and the idea that sharp means threat is contested. A blade reads as a threat by what it is.
- Laws on no known finding - Speedrun Ready, Once Per Save, Emergent Gameplay, The World Reacts, No Artificial Gates, No Asset Flips and Generate, Never Reuse. Each is weighed again, or kept with that said.

## The Gaps

- Relatedness - no law covers bonds with allies, companions, characters or other players ([Parasocial Bonds](../standards/psychology/parasocial-bonds.md)).
- The rhythm of the story - nothing says how intensity rises and falls, or that the story ends on a high ([The Peak-End Rule](../standards/psychology/the-peak-end-rule.md)).
- Curiosity, never compulsion - nothing draws the line between rewarding curiosity and building a compulsion loop ([Variable Reward Schedules](../standards/psychology/variable-reward-schedules.md)).
- Color alone - nothing says state never rests on color alone ([Color Vision Deficiency](../standards/psychology/color-vision-deficiency.md)).
