# Origin

Where the verb comes from, why the verb stayed, and where the three layers come from.

## The Verb

Coentrify comes from Coen, the author, and his habit of structuring and organizing everything, seemingly to excess. His brother coined the verb as a lighthearted joke, with judgment for the excess and respect for the results.

## One Word

The verb stayed because one word does the job. `coentrify` is easy to type, easy to remember, and unusual enough to be easy to find.

## The Intention

The intention is one word that both names the goal and starts the work. Coentrified should mean a well-organized, structured and harmonious codebase.

## Adopt, Configure, Customize

The pattern comes from StandardKit, an open source project of standardized tooling with the guardrails built in. StandardKit came in three layers: defaults that just work, configs to choose a flavor, and overrides to customize. Coentrify carries the same three layers from tooling to a whole way of working. StandardKit itself is no longer maintained.
