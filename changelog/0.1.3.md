# 0.1.3

A template for the Prototype phase page, and lore that starts from the first ideas. A game in Concept gives its Prototype phase page the template's sections, and may open Lore tickets as soon as its ideas are processed.

- 205 standards in 21 modules: one added and five changed
- The Prototype Phase Template: what the prototype tries, the round, the loop mechanism and its balance, what it needs, the questions with the law each tests, and what comes out of it
- Lore starts from Concept on, as soon as the ideas are processed, and the Lore and World Building phases are where it is done
- A phase whose work may start earlier says so, beside the rule that tickets only the current and the next phase
