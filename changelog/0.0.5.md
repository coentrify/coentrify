# 0.0.5

Every Why says why its rule is needed, read twice over every module. Nothing in it is stable yet.

- 196 standards in 21 modules: two renamed, one moved and 94 changed
- A Why says why the rule is needed, where it named the goal or the principle behind it
- The sweep over every Why is done, and a second sweep read every module again against the new bar
- Each workflow says where a finding goes, and Note Issues, Backward-Trace Missing Prerequisites and Sweeps Enumerate Into Tech-Debt First point there
- Stabilize Before Adding holds for any product, so it moved to Tickets Workflow
- Every law and phase of Gamedev holds for every game, or says what it is relevant for
- The conductor is the controller between the director and the agents
- `npm run changelog:standards` writes the list of standards on a version page
