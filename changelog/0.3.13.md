# 0.3.13

A round of filings.

- 290 standards in 23 modules: one added and seven changed
- The DEV Day is a standard of its own in Records, which every dated record links to and a project adopts with any of them. A new Log Append spec picks the request log's page by the DEV day, turning at 04:00, opens a new page with its H1, and writes the time heading. The unlink script lists each removed link that pointed at a section, for the agent to offer that standard. In a project, the request log's shell line gives way to the append script, #106
- The recommendations advise Gate Commits On Exit Codes at least, within the director's pick of Commits, since every project's agent commits and it keeps a red check from landing, #107
- A ticket names its epic and its story by id, `epic: EPIC-12`, so a name used twice never moves a ticket. In a project, every ticket's `epic` and `story` fields move from slugs to ids, the wiki groups by the id, and the hygiene check fails a field that names no epic or story by its id, #108
- The hygiene check fails the page of a phase not yet passed that lacks one of its gate's questions, so an update that changes a gate's questions shows every page that lags. A question left out stays on the page, with the reason as its answer, #109
