# 0.1.1

The first round of changes filed from a project that runs on the package: two new game laws and four sharper ones, filing without hesitation, and an update that runs by itself. A project adds `update` to its `coentrify.json`, `automatic` by default, and rebuilds its coentrify.json check from the changed spec.

- 202 standards in 21 modules: seven changed
- Desire Before Reward and Bands, Not Bullseyes join the game laws
- Always Something To Do keeps a queue of goals at every skill level, Know What Is Coming unlocks nothing before it is introduced, No Hard Edges holds softly for the world, and music may come from a licensed library
- A contributor's setting is the standing go to file, and the agent creates the issue itself. A law for a subset of games is filed too
- The onboarding sets every adopted standard up in full, and puts a part to leave out to the director
- `update` in `coentrify.json` lets a session bring the project up to date by itself once the installed coentrify is newer
- A part of a standard that looks optional carries its own reason, starting with the wiki's lightbox
