# 0.3.4

New flavor layers from a project's sweep.

- 266 standards in 23 modules: five added and one changed
- A game's own strategy can stand at a layer the catalog lacks: `roll-flavors.mjs check` passes it, and names the layer as one to file
- A new layer, Era, at the top: Period Faithful, Mixed Era, Retro Future, Present Day and Timeless
- A new layer, The Secret: Every Theory Is True, The Mundane Truth, One True Theory and Never Confirmed. A layer that does not fit a game is left out and never rolled
- A new layer, Reveal: Shifting World, Late Twist, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, and the director's New Eyes and Quiet Realization, where only the player's view of the same world turns
- A new layer, Conventions: Accepted Artifice, Lampshaded and Explained In The World
- A new layer, Death, whose flavors mix, one per kind of character: Permanent Death, Nobody Really Dies, Respawn and Scripted Deaths Only. A pick can name what it covers after a dash
