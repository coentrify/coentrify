# 0.3.12

A round of filings.

- 289 standards in 23 modules: one added and five changed
- Content is a destination of its own: a detail within a topic, such as a side character, a side quest or a reference, waits in the dump until its content is made, and each design phase goes only as deep as its gate needs. A new Content Template keeps a game's decided content in `docs/content/<kind>/`, an overview with the kind's fields and a file per entry, so the lore stays the full story, #104
- A Lore or Game ticket asks the director only what its skeleton needs: each open point is marked skeleton or detail, and a detail takes the content rule under Veto or waits as Content. At `guided`, a detail is no question, #105
