# 0.3.5

A creative sweep for flavors and patterns.

- 270 standards in 23 modules: one added and one changed
- A new layer, Tone, at the top, with nine main tones that mix as a main tone and accents: Comic, Earnest, Dark, Black Comedy, Satirical, Whimsical, Melancholic, Absurd and Cozy
- A layer's flavors are its main ones, never all there are, and the roll script counts only a heading with an Example line as a flavor
