# 0.3.5

A creative sweep for flavors and patterns.

- 279 standards in 23 modules: ten added and five changed
- A new layer, Tone, at the top, with nine main tones that mix as a main tone and accents: Comic, Earnest, Dark, Black Comedy, Satirical, Whimsical, Melancholic, Absurd and Cozy
- A layer's flavors are its main ones, never all there are, and the roll script counts only a heading with an Example line as a flavor
- A new layer, Kind Of Play, with eight main kinds that mix: Competition, Chance, Make-Believe and Vertigo after Caillois, and Discovery, Creation, Order and Reflection
- A new layer, Story Delivery, with six main channels that mix: Cutscenes, Environmental Storytelling, Found Documents, Dialogue, Narrator and Wordless
- A new layer, Protagonist: Made By The Player, Blank Slate, Defined Hero, Ensemble and No Protagonist, each weighed against Make Yourself
- A new layer, Story Structure, mixing by scope: Hero's Journey, Three Acts, Kishōtenketsu, Episodic, Emergent, Time Loop and Fragmented
- A new law, Recognizable With Tension: a type the player knows at a glance, with a true detail or two that pull against it, lands funny or touching
- A new layer, Plot, after Booker's seven basic plots and the two he adds: Overcoming The Monster, Rags To Riches, The Quest, Voyage And Return, Comedy, Tragedy, Rebirth, Rebellion Against The One and Mystery
- A new layer, Opening: In Medias Res, Ordinary World, Cold Open, Prologue and Straight Into Play
- A new layer, Ending, mixing outcome and form: Triumphant, Bittersweet, Tragic, Open, Homecoming, Multiple Endings and The World Goes On
- A new layer, Choice: Linear Story, Branching, Lasting Choices and Illusion Of Choice, which sets Lasting Impact aside
- A new layer, Morality: Karma Meter, Reputation, Gray Morality, the director's Layered Reputation, No Judgment and Written Morals
