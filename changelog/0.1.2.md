# 0.1.2

The second round of changes filed from a game project: ideas that wait in a dump until their phase is next, three new game laws, and wayfinding. A project on the tickets workflow tickets only the current and the next phase, and moves the idea tickets for later phases into `docs/ideas/dump.md`. A game moves its lore and world docs into `docs/lore/` and `docs/world/`, and its Idea and Outline into `docs/overview.md`.

- 204 standards in 21 modules: two added and ten changed
- Ideas wait in a dump in `docs/ideas/`, with a page per category, and leave it per topic once their phase is next. Work is ticketed for the current and the next phase only, bugs and debt aside
- A game's lore and world docs get folders of their own, and its overview opens with the Idea: the line, the pillars and its Something Special
- References Stay References, No Artificial Gates and Contrast join the game laws
- Wayfinding builds a world the player finds the way in from paths, edges, districts, nodes and landmarks
