# 0.2.1

The game laws checked against what is known about people. Every law names the findings it rests on, in a new Psychology module with checked sources, and the laws grow by two parts written from the director's view, Companions and Values.

- 250 standards in 22 modules: 44 added and fourteen changed
- The Psychology module holds one page per finding: its sources, how solid it is and where it stops. A project without the module keeps the words of every reference
- Every law ends with the findings it rests on, and a law that claimed more than its finding gives was reworded or given a better finding
- Companions holds nine laws for characters of their own, bonded by shared circumstance
- Values aims for truth and goodness, with compulsion always considered and no real money in play
- New laws elsewhere: Natural Breaks, Cadence, Every Sense Carries It, In Harmony, Embedded Relevance, and Tracks, Never Instructs
- Migration: a game that adopted Never Wait names Always Something To Do, which took it in
- Migration: a game that adopted Alive App, Static Actions names Alive World, Static Actions
- Directing, a guide for the director, joins the root pages
- Tickets come only for the current and the next phase, in every page that describes the workflow
- A rename that changes meaning greps the old wording too
- An example the director gives to make a point stays out of a law
