# Baby Schema

Baby-like features, such as a large head, a round face and big eyes, look cute and move people to take care. The more of them, the stronger the pull.

## Why

A weak or helpless character, such as an abandoned puppy, draws the player in to protect it.

## The Evidence

- Glocker, Langleben, Ruparel, Loughead and Gur 2009, Baby schema in infant faces induces cuteness perception and motivation for caretaking in adults, Ethology 115(3), 257-263, [doi:10.1111/j.1439-0310.2008.01603.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1439-0310.2008.01603.x). Faces edited to hold more baby features were rated cuter, and people were more willing to care for them.
- How solid: solid, from Lorenz's idea to controlled tests.

## Limits

- The test used human infant faces. Whether animals and drawn characters work the same way, it did not test.
- Helplessness that keeps demanding care turns into a chore, so the care stays the player's choice.
