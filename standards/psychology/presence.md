# Presence

Presence is the sense of being in a place while the body is somewhere else. It needs attention on that place, and grows with what the senses take in, with a world that answers the person, and with the person's own pull to get involved.

## Why

A world that moves, sounds and answers the player's actions holds the player's attention, and the player feels there.

## The Evidence

- Witmer and Singer 1998, Measuring presence in virtual environments: a presence questionnaire, Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments 7(3), 225-240, [doi:10.1162/105474698565686](https://doi.org/10.1162/105474698565686). Presence rests on directed attention, on what the senses take in, on a setting that invites involvement, and on the person's own tendency to get involved. Involvement comes from attention on a coherent set of stimuli, and among the factors they proposed is a world the person can change. People with more simulator sickness reported less presence.
- Cummings and Bailenson 2016, How immersive is enough? A meta-analysis of the effect of immersive technology on user presence, Media Psychology 19(2), 272-309, [doi:10.1080/15213269.2015.1015740](https://doi.org/10.1080/15213269.2015.1015740). Immersive technology raised presence by a medium amount. A view that follows the person's own movement did more than better pictures or sound.
- Slater 1999, Measuring presence: a response to the Witmer and Singer presence questionnaire, Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments 8(5), 560-565, [doi:10.1162/105474699566477](https://doi.org/10.1162/105474699566477). Doubts that a questionnaire captures presence.
- How solid: presence itself is well established. Its causes are proposed more than tested one by one, and how to measure it is disputed.

## Limits

- The studies are mostly of virtual reality with a headset. A game on a screen is less immersive by its hardware, and carrying the factors over is an extrapolation.
- No study isolates a slowly moving world, a world that reacts, or a coherent style. That those feed presence is reasoned from the factors.
