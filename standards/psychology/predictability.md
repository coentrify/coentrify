# Predictability

A danger that gives a sign ahead is feared only while the sign shows. A danger that can strike at any time keeps fear up all the time, and leaves nowhere feeling safe.

## Why

A risk the player can read leaves the rest of the world safe, and a risk that can come from nowhere keeps the player on guard everywhere.

## The Evidence

- Seligman 1968, Chronic fear produced by unpredictable electric shock, Journal of Comparative and Physiological Psychology 66(2), 402-411, [doi:10.1037/h0026355](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026355). Rats warned of a shock feared only the warning. Unwarned, they stayed afraid throughout.
- Weiss 1970, Somatic effects of predictable and unpredictable shock, Psychosomatic Medicine 32(4), 397-408, [doi:10.1097/00006842-197007000-00008](https://doi.org/10.1097/00006842-197007000-00008). The same shocks with a warning did less harm to the body, such as fewer stomach ulcers.
- Schmitz and Grillon 2012, Assessing fear and anxiety in humans using the threat of predictable and unpredictable aversive events (the NPU-threat test), Nature Protocols 7(3), 527-532, [doi:10.1038/nprot.2012.001](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2012.001). In people, a predictable threat brings fear at its cue, and an unpredictable one a lasting anxiety.
- How solid: solid, from animals first and confirmed in people.

## Limits

- The research uses real pain and threat. A game's danger is chosen and mild, and some players seek out the tension of the unknown.
- It covers seeing a danger coming. Steering it is [Controllability](controllability.md).

## The Laws On It

- [Fair Surprise](../gamedev/game-design-laws-world.md#fair-surprise) - after the first time a risk is readable, and one that can never be read is a dice roll

- [A Loss Is Grieved](../gamedev/game-design-laws-companions.md#a-loss-is-grieved) - a companion is lost only to a risk the player could see coming
