# Rewards And Feedback

An expected reward for something a person already enjoys lowers the wish to do it freely, and so does feedback that feels controlling. Feedback that informs raises it. Praise helps when it is sincere, about what the person did, and free of pressure.

## Why

What a game says when the player succeeds can feed the player's own interest or replace it, depending on whether it informs or judges and pushes.

## The Evidence

- Lepper, Greene and Nisbett 1973, Undermining children's intrinsic interest with extrinsic reward, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 28(1), 129-137, [doi:10.1037/h0035519](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0035519). Children who expected an award for drawing later drew less in free time. A surprise award did no harm.
- Deci, Koestner and Ryan 1999, A meta-analytic review of experiments examining the effects of extrinsic rewards on intrinsic motivation, Psychological Bulletin 125(6), 627-668, [doi:10.1037/0033-2909.125.6.627](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.125.6.627). Expected tangible rewards lowered intrinsic motivation, and positive verbal feedback raised it.
- Henderlong and Lepper 2002, The effects of praise on children's intrinsic motivation: a review and synthesis, Psychological Bulletin 128(5), 774-795, [doi:10.1037/0033-2909.128.5.774](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.5.774). Praise helps when sincere, about the process, and free of pressure, and harms when it judges the person or conveys low expectations.
- Meyer et al. 1979, The informational value of evaluative behavior: influences of praise and blame on perceptions of ability, Journal of Educational Psychology 71(2), 259-268, [doi:10.1037/0022-0663.71.2.259](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.71.2.259). Praise for an easy success led observers to judge the praised person's ability low. Replications are mixed.
- How solid: solid, with the size of the effect still debated.

## Limits

- Several studies found girls and women more sensitive to praise that controls or judges the person. In mixed feedback, girls' interest fell where boys' did not (Kast and Connor 1988, [doi:10.1177/0146167288143010](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167288143010)), and praise of the person lowered girls' motivation and left boys' as it was (Henderlong Corpus and Lepper 2007, [doi:10.1080/01443410601159852](https://doi.org/10.1080/01443410601159852)). The evidence is mixed, and mostly from children.

## The Laws On It

- [Adopt, Repeat, Confirm (ARC)](../gamedev/game-design-laws-guidance.md#adopt-repeat-confirm-arc) - a failed Confirm teaches through its result alone
- [No Yellow Paint](../gamedev/game-design-laws-guidance.md#no-yellow-paint) - a line states what happened, and never judges or pushes
