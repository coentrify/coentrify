# Narrative Transportation

A person can be absorbed into a story, attention, imagery and feeling together. The more absorbed, the more the person takes on the story's view and likes its characters.

## Why

A story the player is absorbed in carries the game, and an invented world does that as well as a true one.

## The Evidence

- Green and Brock 2000, The role of transportation in the persuasiveness of public narratives, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 79(5), 701-721, [doi:10.1037/0022-3514.79.5.701](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.79.5.701). Labeling a story fact or fiction made no difference.
- van Laer, de Ruyter, Visconti and Wetzels 2014, The extended transportation-imagery model, Journal of Consumer Research 40(5), 797-817, [doi:10.1086/673383](https://doi.org/10.1086/673383). A meta-analysis.
- How solid: solid, with modest effects.

## Limits

- The research uses stories that are read or watched. It never compared showing with telling, or playing with watching.
