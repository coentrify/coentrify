# Color Vision Deficiency

About 8 in 100 men and 4 in 1,000 women of European ancestry see red and green poorly, and rates differ between populations. A difference in brightness often survives where a difference in hue does not.

## Why

State shown by color alone is invisible to a part of the players.

## The Evidence

- Birch 2012, Worldwide prevalence of red-green color deficiency, Journal of the Optical Society of America A 29(3), 313-320, [doi:10.1364/JOSAA.29.000313](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.29.000313).
- WCAG 2.2, criterion 1.4.1 Use of Color, [w3.org/TR/WCAG22](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#use-of-color). Color is never the only means of conveying information.
- How solid: solid.

## Limits

- The 8 in 100 holds for men of European ancestry, never for people in general.
