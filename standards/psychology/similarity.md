# Similarity

People are drawn to those who are like them, in attitudes, goals and situation. Similarity that is perceived counts once people know each other.

## Why

A companion with goals or a situation like the player's is easy to bond with.

## The Evidence

- Montoya, Horton and Kirchner 2008, Is actual similarity necessary for attraction? A meta-analysis of actual and perceived similarity, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships 25(6), 889-922, [doi:10.1177/0265407508096700](https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407508096700). Across 313 studies, both actual and perceived similarity went with attraction. In existing relationships, only perceived similarity did.
- How solid: solid.

## Limits

- It covers similarity. Conditions that complement each other are about working together, which it does not cover.
- The studies paired real people, so carrying them to a game character rests on [Parasocial Bonds](parasocial-bonds.md).
