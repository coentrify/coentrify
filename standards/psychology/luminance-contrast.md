# Luminance Contrast

Text is legible when its brightness differs enough from what lies behind it. Reading speed holds over a wide range of contrast, and falls steeply near a threshold, sooner for small text and for weaker eyes.

## Why

A glow behind a text is the text's background, so it needs enough brightness contrast with the letters.

## The Evidence

- Legge, Rubin and Luebker 1987, Psychophysics of reading V: the role of contrast in normal vision, Vision Research 27(7), 1165-1177, [doi:10.1016/0042-6989(87)90028-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0042-6989(87)90028-9).
- WCAG 2.2, criterion 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), [w3.org/TR/WCAG22](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum). A ratio of 4.5 to 1 for body text, with a margin for weaker vision.
- How solid: solid.

## Limits

- Contrast here means brightness. A glow of another hue at a similar brightness blurs the letters.
- A soft glow fades toward its edge, so contrast is measured where the glow is weakest.
