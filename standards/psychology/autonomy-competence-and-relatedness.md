# Autonomy, Competence And Relatedness

Self-Determination Theory names three basic needs: autonomy, acting by one's own will, competence, feeling effective, and relatedness, feeling connected to others. Where a game meets them, players enjoy it more and want to come back.

## Why

The three needs are the best supported account of why people play at all, so most laws about the player lean on one of them.

## The Evidence

- Ryan and Deci 2000, Self-determination theory and the facilitation of intrinsic motivation, social development, and well-being, American Psychologist 55(1), 68-78, [doi:10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68). The three needs together.
- Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski 2006, The motivational pull of video games: a self-determination theory approach, Motivation and Emotion 30(4), 344-360, [doi:10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-006-9051-8). Felt autonomy and competence predicted enjoyment and the wish to play again, and relatedness did in online play.
- Vansteenkiste, Ryan and Soenens 2020, Basic psychological need theory, Motivation and Emotion 44(1), 1-31, [doi:10.1007/s11031-019-09818-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11031-019-09818-1). The current state of the theory.
- Amabile, DeJong and Lepper 1976, Effects of externally imposed deadlines on subsequent intrinsic motivation, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 34(1), 92-98, [doi:10.1037/0022-3514.34.1.92](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.34.1.92). Working to a deadline lowered the wish to go on with a task for its own sake.
- Deci, Eghrari, Patrick and Leone 1994, Facilitating internalization: the self-determination theory perspective, Journal of Personality 62(1), 119-142, [doi:10.1111/j.1467-6494.1994.tb00797.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6494.1994.tb00797.x). A meaningful reason for a dull task, with choice stressed over control, helped people take it on as their own.
- How solid: solid, mostly from surveys, with few game experiments.

## Limits

- Autonomy means acting by one's own will, which differs from having many options. More choice is not more autonomy.
- The deadline study used puzzles in a lab. Reading a game's daily clock as a deadline is an extrapolation.
- The study of reasons used a dull lab task. A reason the world gives for a gate is the same idea, by inference.
- In games, relatedness has been measured with other players. Whether a bond with a character counts is still open (Tyack and Mekler 2020, [doi:10.1145/3313831.3376723](https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376723)).

## The Laws On It

- [Full Control](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#full-control), [Respect](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#respect), [No Try Hard](../gamedev/game-design-laws-progress.md#no-try-hard) and [Lasting Impact](../gamedev/game-design-laws-progress.md#lasting-impact) - autonomy
- [Natural Breaks](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#natural-breaks) - the player's own pace, with a breather from the clock
- [Speedrun Ready](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#speedrun-ready) and [Once Per Save](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#once-per-save) - the returning player's own pace
- [Emergent Gameplay](../gamedev/game-design-laws-progress.md#emergent-gameplay) - a play style of the player's own
- [No Artificial Gates](../gamedev/game-design-laws-world.md#no-artificial-gates) - a limit comes with a reason the world gives
- [Doable 100%](../gamedev/game-design-laws-progress.md#doable-100) and [Loop First](../gamedev/game-design-laws-experience.md#loop-first) - competence
- [Bands, Not Bullseyes](../gamedev/game-design-laws-progress.md#bands-not-bullseyes) - a beginner scrapes through and any method counts, competence and autonomy
- [Only What Matters](../gamedev/game-design-laws-companions.md#only-what-matters) - the player decides what to spend play on
- [Helps, Never Takes Over](../gamedev/game-design-laws-companions.md#helps-never-takes-over) - the player solves what they can alone
- [Tracks, Never Instructs](../gamedev/game-design-laws-guidance.md#tracks-never-instructs) - a task comes with a reason the player cares about
- [Compulsion Is Considered](../gamedev/game-design-laws-values.md#compulsion-is-considered) - compulsion only from blocked paths, always on purpose
