# Redundant Signals

A signal carried on two senses at once is caught faster than on either alone. People also differ in what they take in best: perception varies with acuity and with training, and someone missing a sense leans on the others and sharpens them.

## Why

State and meaning carried on several senses reach every player, whatever each takes in best.

## The Evidence

- Miller 1982, Divided attention: evidence for coactivation with redundant signals, Cognitive Psychology 14(2), 247-279, [doi:10.1016/0010-0285(82)90010-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(82)90010-X). A light and a tone together were answered faster than either alone, faster than a race between the two explains.
- Wiedenbeck 1999, The use of icons and labels in an end user application program: an empirical study of learning and retention, Behaviour and Information Technology 18(2), 68-82, [doi:10.1080/014492999119129](https://doi.org/10.1080/014492999119129). Icons alone were harder to learn at first than labels or icons with labels, and users preferred icons with labels.
- Mayer and Massa 2003, Three facets of visual and verbal learners: cognitive ability, cognitive style, and learning preference, Journal of Educational Psychology 95(4), 833-846, [doi:10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.833](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.833). People differ in visual and verbal ability, style and preference, three separate traits.
- Lessard, Paré, Lepore and Lassonde 1998, Early-blind human subjects localize sound sources better than sighted subjects, Nature 395(6699), 278-280, [doi:10.1038/26228](https://doi.org/10.1038/26228).
- Goldstone 1998, Perceptual learning, Annual Review of Psychology 49, 585-612, [doi:10.1146/annurev.psych.49.1.585](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.49.1.585). Practice tunes perception to what matters in a task, and makes once indistinguishable things distinct.
- Kraus and Chandrasekaran 2010, Music training for the development of auditory skills, Nature Reviews Neuroscience 11(8), 599-605, [doi:10.1038/nrn2882](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2882). Musicians hear finer differences in sound.
- How solid: the redundant signals effect and perceptual learning are solid. The icon study is a single study.

## Limits

- Matching teaching to a person's preferred sense, the idea of learning styles, did not improve learning when tested (Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer and Bjork 2008, Learning styles: concepts and evidence, Psychological Science in the Public Interest 9(3), 105-119, [doi:10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x), and Rogowsky, Calhoun and Tallal 2015, [doi:10.1037/a0037478](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037478)). Preferences are real, and a message on every sense serves each of them.
- Acuity and training shape what a person picks up fast, such as a designer reading icons or a musician hearing a cue. Whether a stated preference does is not settled.
