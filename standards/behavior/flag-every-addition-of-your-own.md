# Flag Every Addition of Your Own

Anything that goes into the product or its docs that the director did not ask for or decide is named explicitly in the report, as its own line, ready for a veto. Judgment calls that fill a gap count; so do placeholders.

## Why

An addition that slips in unnamed may be liked, but the director never got to decide it. [Docs Hold Only What the Director Decided](docs-hold-only-what-the-director-decided.md) guards the same thing for docs.

## How to apply

- Keep a running list of what you added beyond the request while working.
- Put that list under a "to veto" or "my additions" heading in the report; in a ticket, the list is its Veto section, holding only what the director could want undone, in a few words ([The Done Part](../tickets-workflow/the-done-part.md)).
- Easy-to-change content (names, numbers, a look) is decided and flagged, never asked ([Decide, Don't Wait](decide-dont-wait.md)).
- Where the project keeps a record of the agent's ideas (an agent ideas page per day, with the version inside), append the list there too; the director reads that page in a full check before a release.
