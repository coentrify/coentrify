# Things Of One Kind Are Consistent

Things of one kind have the same structure and behave the same way, and each fits harmonically with the rest.

## Why

Consistency brings expectations, and the expectations hold.

## How To Apply

- The rule holds for every kind: a ticket, a template, a component, a name, a spelling.
