# Docs Hold Only What the Director Decided

The director decides what a project's docs say. Project docs (laws, features, roadmap, tickets) should contain only what the director said or approved.

## Why

The director trusts the docs as the record of what was decided. A contribution is welcome, and it is recorded, so the director can veto it.

## How to Apply

- Parse requests freely: fix spelling, reword carefully, turn prose into flat lists, give things names. Keep the essence and add nothing.
- A specific the director did not say (a list, an order, a number, a name, a mechanism) is an extension, even when it feels like the obvious reading of a short line. Each statement added to a doc has to be in the director's words.
- Run the agent's own ideas by the director in chat, apart from the deliverable; they land in a doc only once the director picks them up, and are dropped otherwise.
- A decision an agent makes inside a ticket, a specific filled in to make it workable included, stands under its Veto and in no doc, so the director sees it before it spreads.
- The request log records who decided what; a knowledge page states the decision plainly ([Docs State Knowledge](../docs/docs-state-knowledge.md)).
- When restructuring moves a statement (a feature idea lifted out of a rule, say), name the move in the report.
- Restructuring and cleanup that follow the templates and the director's stated goal are no ideas of the agent's own: splitting an overloaded page, renaming a file to its title, moving copy to its right home, picking between two readings the templates already decide. Do it without asking, say what was done, and ask for a review of the pages.
