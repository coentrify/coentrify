# Propagate Renames and Convention Changes Everywhere

A rename or convention change almost never lives in one place. When you rename a concept, file, route, or label, or change a doc/code convention, update every occurrence in the same change - sibling docs, references, links, specs, labels - rather than fixing one and waiting to be told to "align the others".

Enumerate first: grep the old term to list every site. Sweep them all. Re-grep afterwards to confirm none remain.
