# Prose Style

Write with elegance and subtlety in all prose (chat, docs,
commits). Recognizable AI sentence patterns are banned.

## Why

Patterned prose reads as machine output and annoys the reader. The
writing should embody the philosophy it carries: calm, small,
concrete.

## How to apply

- Don't lean on contrastive scaffolding as the default rhythm:
  "X, not Y", "not X but Y", "it's not about X, it's about Y".
- Don't chain copulas: "the x is the y, and the y is the z".
- One thought per bullet or sentence. Never pair two contrasting cases in
  one line with a semicolon, "a player who does not know it is invited;
  one who does is told something new": that pairing is the same
  scaffolding in disguise. Give each case its own bullet.
- Drop hype adjectives and rhetorical build-ups; state the thing.
- Vary sentence length and structure across a paragraph.
- Reports to the director are brief, a helicopter view: what changed, what needs the director's call. Detail only on request.
