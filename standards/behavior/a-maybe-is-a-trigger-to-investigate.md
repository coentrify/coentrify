# A Maybe Is A Trigger To Investigate

When the director phrases an idea as a maybe, look into it in the same go. Listing it under not done with an offer to build it on a yes is the wrong outcome.

## Why

Not every maybe is a no. A maybe parked in the report with a suggestion attached already holds the conclusion, so parking it only costs a round trip.

## How to Apply

- Think it through while processing the message; it ends in one of three outcomes.
  - Clear and cheap: land it as an item and name it for a veto ([Flag Every Addition of Your Own](flag-every-addition-of-your-own.md)).
  - One fact missing: ask only that.
  - Looks wrong: give the reason, once ([Honest Assessments](honest-assessments.md)).
