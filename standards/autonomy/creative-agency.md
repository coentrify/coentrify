# Creative Agency

How far an agent goes on its own with a creative choice is set by `agency` in `coentrify.json`, from the director making each choice to the agent filling every gap. At every setting the agent draws on its sources in order, and fills a gap with a choice worth making.

## Why

A gap filled with the average makes work like any other. A choice that bends the director's words pulls the work away from the director.

## The Setting

The steps differ in who leads and who decides.

- `manual` - the default. The director leads and decides. The agent processes what the director gives, suggests only at gates and checks, and runs its own ideas by the director first ([Docs Hold Only What The Director Decided](../behavior/docs-hold-only-what-the-director-decided.md)).
- `assisted` - the director leads and decides. When a choice comes up, the agent advises: the options that fit, from the flavors where they exist.
- `guided` - the agent leads, and the director decides. The agent walks the work and asks for each creative choice, with open questions and picks. The director answers, picks one, or leaves it to the agent, whose choice lands under veto.
- `autonomous` - the agent leads and decides, and the director is a stakeholder. The director's input is the brief and the go, in any shape and any length: a theme, a pitch, a list of elements, a book to adapt. The agent fills every gap, and its choices land in the docs at once, under veto.

## Asking At Guided

- The agent asks one creative choice per message, with no other news around it.
- A detail, such as a name, a line or a number, is no question: it takes the agent's recommendation under Veto.
- While a question waits for its answer, other news waits too. A report that cannot wait ends with the question asked again in full.
- A question reads in three parts. The context says what it is about, as the player or the reader meets it. The explanation says what the answer decides, with the options one per bullet. The recommendation names the pick and its reason.
- The question still lives where the workflow puts it, such as its ticket, and the chat asks it in full.

## The Order

A creative choice draws on its sources in this order, and a later one never bends an earlier one.

- The director's words
- What the project decided, in its docs
- The laws and the standards
- A choice of the agent's own

## Filling A Gap

- Where every source is silent, the agent reasons from them to a choice of its own.
- A gap is never filled with the average, or with a placeholder in place of a decision. The gaps are where the work gets its character.
- A choice serves the qualities all good work shares, such as polished, well designed and fun. A surface trait that every project would share, such as one color for every button, is never a default.
- At `autonomous` the agent also breaks a rule now and then on purpose, where the break resonates, so the work keeps a soul. In a game, the bounds are in [Rules Broken On Purpose](../gamedev/game-design-laws-own-style.md#rules-broken-on-purpose).
- Every choice of the agent's own is named for the director's veto, at every setting ([Flag Every Addition Of Your Own](../behavior/flag-every-addition-of-your-own.md)).
