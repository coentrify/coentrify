# Game Phases: Design

The second stage of a game ([Game Phases](game-phases.md)). Design is convergent: the game is decided, on paper and in a first playable.

## Lore

The skeleton of the lore, the red thread of the world: how the story starts, how it ends and how its parts connect, with the main questline, its key people, its secret, and the side lines the main one cannot do without. The main parts are there and fit. Not every dialog and every person: side characters, side quests and references are Content, made later ([Content Template](content-template.md)). Done When: the lore holds that framework and the director has approved it. Its work is Lore tickets, which may start from Concept on ([Game Work Workflow](game-work-workflow.md#design-ticket-types)). Gate: do the main parts fit together?

## World Building

The world itself: its places, how they connect and how big it is. A world the player finds the way in is built for it ([Wayfinding](wayfinding.md)). Its work is Lore tickets. Gate: does the world hold what the lore and the gameplay need?

## Vertical Slice

Optional, for a game whose look is the bigger unknown: one small part of the world that looks, feels and plays the way the game should, with the style sample, a machine or a minigame, and the core loop in it. Only what it holds is specced, so the rest stays open to try out, and what it teaches feeds Gameplay and the style. It stays internal, never a demo. Gate: does it look, feel and play the way the game should?

## Gameplay

The main gameplay: the core loop and the main mechanics. Its work is Game tickets. Gate: is the main gameplay decided?

## First Playable

One of everything, rough, in the real project: one of each kind of content and one of each mechanic, playable together. Game tickets design it, and the Feature tickets they lead to build it. Gate: does the core hold up when played?

- A first playable is never a demo. A demo is public, and the first playable stays internal.
- It shows that the core holds up when played, and that the project builds end to end.
- Beside it stands a style sample, or the vertical slice's: a few assets at target quality, made in the test world, which set the look and show what a part costs ([Asset Layers](asset-layers.md#the-test-world)). The look is written into the game's style, which every asset list takes ([Asset Layers](asset-layers.md)).
- Nothing else is polished, since polish waits for Alpha.

## Game Design

The world translated into the game's levels and features, represented by docs and tickets. Nothing is built. Done When: every part of the world has its levels and features in docs and filed tickets, refined once their phase comes, and the director has approved the design as a whole. Its work is Game tickets. Gate: is the whole game on paper? Its check questions weigh the mix too ([Gate Check Questions](gate-check-questions.md#design)).

## The Order

- Lore, World Building and Gameplay lean on each other.
- When Design starts, the agent asks which is the bigger unknown, the look or the gameplay, and recommends a Vertical Slice where it is the look.
- Each phase goes only as deep as its gate needs, and leaves the details as Content ([Processing The Ideas](../tickets-workflow/processing-the-ideas.md)).
- The skeleton they decide is the frame: decided once and kept stable, so content fills its gaps later.
- The order on this page is the default, and a phase gate may send the game back a phase.
