# Game Flavors: Conventions

Whether a game explains its own artifice, such as its edges, its routines and its respawns. [Rules Broken On Purpose](game-design-laws-own-style.md#rules-broken-on-purpose) covers a rare break of the frame, and the flavor sets how the frame is held all game long. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Accepted Artifice

The conventions are never explained, and nobody minds.

Example: an invisible wall at the edge of the map that nobody mentions.

Conflicts: Lampshaded and Explained In The World.

## Lampshaded

Characters notice the conventions and joke about them.

Example: a guard jokes that he walks the same loop all day.

Conflicts: Accepted Artifice and Explained In The World.

## Explained In The World

Each convention has a cause in the world, often one the story uncovers.

Example: the map ends at a sea too cold to cross, and a sailor says why.

Conflicts: Accepted Artifice and Lampshaded.
