# Game Flavors: Kind Of Play

What the player is doing at heart, after Roger Caillois's four kinds of play and the kinds games have found since. These are the eight main kinds, never all there are. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## How Kinds Mix

- Kinds mix: a game picks a main kind, and accents where they fit, each naming its scope after a dash: `- Kind Of Play: Vertigo - main`, `- Kind Of Play: Competition - the races`.
- Each kind runs from free improvisation to rule-bound effort, Caillois's paidia and ludus, and a game sets where it sits.
- Escape is no kind of its own: every kind offers it, and the Cozy tone and Make-Believe carry it most.

## Competition

Skill measured against others or the game, in a fair contest. Caillois's agôn.

Example: a sword duel where the better reader of tells wins.

Weighs: mastery and fairness matter, and the less skilled are shut out, so it stays within [No Try Hard](game-design-laws-progress.md#no-try-hard).

## Chance

Fate decides, and the thrill is in the luck. Caillois's alea.

Example: a market whose prices are drawn from a deck each dawn.

Weighs: excitement and replay, and an equalizer, and it can feel unfair. Never with real money, and compulsion is always weighed ([Game Design Laws: Values](game-design-laws-values.md)).

## Make-Believe

Becoming someone else, living a role. Caillois's mimicry.

Example: running a small-town bakery and chatting with the regulars.

Weighs: deep immersion and identity, and it needs a deep world and a slower pace.

## Vertigo

The rush of speed, spin and pleasant disorder. Caillois's ilinx.

Example: skiing down a mountain that crumbles behind you.

Weighs: a physical thrill, and it tires, and can make some players sick, so a setting for less motion helps.

## Discovery

Curiosity and exploring, the easy fun of finding out.

Example: an island where every cave hides a different small mystery.

Weighs: wonder that pulls the player on, and it needs a world dense enough to reward looking ([Exploration Is Rewarded](game-design-laws-experience.md#exploration-is-rewarded)).

## Creation

Building, crafting, automating and expressing: the player makes the world their own.

Example: a valley the player turns into a working mill town, wheel by wheel.

Weighs: a deep sense of ownership, and the player sets their own pace.

## Order

Cleaning up, sorting and restoring: making a mess right.

Example: an attic of a lost relative's things, sorted box by box.

Weighs: a calm satisfaction, and little tension.

## Reflection

Contemplation and meaning: the game as a space to think.

Example: a slow walk along a shore while a voice recalls a life.

Weighs: it stays with the player, and there is little to do.

## Sources

- Caillois, Les jeux et les hommes, 1958, in English Man, Play and Games, 1961, for the first four kinds and the paidia to ludus axis.
- Huizinga, Homo Ludens, 1938, on play as a world apart.
- Lazzaro, Why We Play Games: Four Keys to More Emotion Without Story, 2004, for the easy fun of Discovery.
- Public games, as references only: FIFA for Competition, Factorio for Creation, Unpacking for Order, Journey for Reflection.
