# Visual Design

The visual rules a drawn scene gets wrong on the first try. A project's visual checklist points here, so a ticket's Visual Quality only adds what is its own ([Ticket Template](../tickets-workflow/ticket-template.md)).

## Why

Perspective is what drawn scenes miss most often on the first try, and every round of feedback on it costs the director's time and tokens.

## Perspective

- One horizon and its vanishing points per scene; every object is drawn in them.
- Verticals stay vertical, and the top and bottom edges of a standing object run to the vanishing point, never tilted on their own.
- A space seen inside another, such as a room through a doorway, takes the outer room's vanishing point.
- Repeated elements in depth (lights, tiles, a ceiling grid) shrink and converge together, their lines meeting in one point.
- A pattern on a floor or a ceiling is foreshortened with its surface.
- The check before landing: the scene's perspective lines drawn over a capture, every edge tested against them.
