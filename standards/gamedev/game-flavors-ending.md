# Game Flavors: Ending

How the game ends, and what the player is left with. An experience is remembered by its peak and its end ([The Peak-End Rule](../psychology/the-peak-end-rule.md)), so the ending weighs on the whole game. These are the seven main endings, never all there are. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## How Endings Mix

- An ending's outcome and its form mix: a Homecoming can be Triumphant or Bittersweet.
- A game with several endings names an outcome for each: `- Ending: Bittersweet - the quiet ending`.
- The laws hold over every ending: there is always an end, with a way back in, and the game ends on a high ([Start And End](game-design-laws-progress.md#start-and-end), [As Long As It Is Interesting](game-design-laws-progress.md#as-long-as-it-is-interesting)).

## Triumphant

The goal is won, and the world is better for it.

Example: the dam breaks open, and the dry valley turns green the next spring.

Weighs: satisfying closure, and it can feel simple.

## Bittersweet

The goal is won, at a cost the player feels. The cost can be the conflict itself: with the danger gone, part of the fun goes with it.

Example: the plague is cured, and the doctor who found the cure is not among the cured.

Weighs: it lingers and feels grown-up, and it can read as punishment for the player's effort.

## Tragic

The fall completes, and the loss stands.

Example: the king wins the war and finds the throne room empty.

Weighs: powerful, and it can feel unfair after hours of play, so its closure is a must.

## Open

Questions stay open, and the player decides what it meant.

Example: the ship sails into the fog, and no one says where.

Weighs: resonance and talk afterward, and it frustrates players who want answers.

## Homecoming

Back where it began, the place the same, the hero or the people changed. The player did not get what they wanted, and got what they needed.

Example: the player walks back into the bakery, and the regulars are still arguing about the weather.

Weighs: closure through contrast with the opening, quietly, and it disappoints a cynic, or falls flat when the need is weak.

## Multiple Endings

The player's choices lead to different endings.

Example: whether the town keeps its mill or its forest depends on whom the player helped.

Weighs: choices that matter ([Lasting Impact](game-design-laws-progress.md#lasting-impact)), and each ending matters less the more there are, so a few variants keep their worth, or one stands as the true ending.

## The World Goes On

The story ends, and play carries on after it.

Example: the credits roll after the festival, and the next morning the shop opens as usual.

Weighs: the player can stay, and a world without its conflict can feel empty, since the danger made part of the fun.
