# Game Flavors: Protagonist

Who the player is in the game. [Make Yourself](game-design-laws-story.md#make-yourself) asks that the player can represent themselves, so a flavor that sets it aside says so, and a game that picks it leaves the law out with its reason. These are the five main ones, never all there are. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Made By The Player

The player shapes the character's look and what they stand for, as Make Yourself asks.

Example: a newcomer to a harbor town, whose face, voice and clothes the player shapes.

Weighs: identification and ownership, and the story cannot lean on who the hero is.

Conflicts: Blank Slate, Defined Hero and No Protagonist.

## Blank Slate

A silent, barely described hero the player projects into. It half fits Make Yourself: room to project, and no look of the player's own.

Example: a silent courier whose face always hides under a hood.

Weighs: room to project, and a hero who feels hollow in conversation.

Conflicts: Made By The Player, Defined Hero and No Protagonist.

## Defined Hero

A written character with a face, a voice and a past. It sets Make Yourself aside.

Example: a retired detective with a bad knee and a daughter who won't call back.

Weighs: strong character and story, and the player plays someone else.

Conflicts: Made By The Player, Blank Slate and No Protagonist.

## Ensemble

Several characters the player switches between, made or defined. It sets Make Yourself aside where the characters are written.

Example: four siblings running the family farm, each one playable.

Weighs: range and more than one perspective, and less attachment to any one character.

Conflicts: No Protagonist.

## No Protagonist

The player is an unseen hand, a mayor or a god, with no character of their own. Make Yourself is only relevant for a player character, so it falls outside the law.

Example: the town council, deciding where the roads go.

Weighs: overview and control, and no one to identify with.

Conflicts: Made By The Player, Blank Slate, Defined Hero and Ensemble.
