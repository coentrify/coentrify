# Game Flavors: Naming

How a game's characters are named. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Parody Names

The cast of a level is named as parodies of famous characters from the level's genre, so a player who knows the genre smiles at each name.

Example: a detective level with an inspector named Sherlock Bones.

Conflicts: Serious Names.

## Serious Names

The names fit the cultures and the era of the world, played straight, so they carry the world's weight.

Example: a medieval village whose people carry the names of that time and place.

Conflicts: Parody Names.

## Personal Circle Names

The cast carries the first names of people from the maker's own life, often paired with a trait of theirs, and never a full name. It is an in-joke the player meets as plain names.

Example: a bartender named after the developer's brother.

## Named By Type

Characters go by what they are, such as the guard or the baker, or stay unnamed, so the names never pull focus.

Example: a guard, a baker and a ferryman, each called by their trade.
