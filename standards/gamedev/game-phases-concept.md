# Game Phases: Concept

The first stage of a game ([Game Phases](game-phases.md)). Concept is divergent: everything can change and nothing is wrong.

## Idea

The opening of `docs/overview.md`: the game in one line, its pillars and its Something Special. A pillar is something the game does not hold without. Something Special is the little extra that sets the game apart: not essential, the cherry on top, such as running references and parodies ([Game Work Workflow](game-work-workflow.md#terms)). Gate: does the line excite?

## Gathering

Braindumps, a rant room, brainstorming. Gathering never closes: ideas keep coming, and land in the dump ([Processing The Ideas](../tickets-workflow/processing-the-ideas.md)). Gate: does the director call the outline?

## Outline

The rest of `docs/overview.md` during Concept, made from the ideas: the story, the world and the core loop, each in outline. What the prototype tries stands on the Prototype phase page, a plan that leaves when it is done. Gate: does it say what to prototype?

## Prototype

Trying things: a greybox, the outline and the size of the map, features. A prototype is thrown away. Gate: is the core loop fun in a greybox? ([Loop First](game-design-laws-experience.md#loop-first))

## How To Apply

- Concept is a dump of ideas, and unorganized by nature. Its material is parsed from the director's prompts and brainstorms into `docs/ideas/`.
- The phases of Concept may overlap and loop back.
- A phase page holds its goal, its gate and its Done When, never the content itself ([Work Tracking](../tickets-workflow/work-tracking.md#phases)).
- From Design on, the overview stays the short functional description of the game, and the decided docs take over the detail ([Docs Folder Structure](../docs/docs-folder-structure.md#knowledge)).
- Concept's gathering is logged, and needs no tickets. A prototype is tracked by its one ticket ([Prototype Mode Is Speed First](../behavior/prototype-mode-is-speed-first.md)).
