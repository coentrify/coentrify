# Game Phases: Concept

The first stage of a game ([Game Phases](game-phases.md)). Concept is divergent: everything can change and nothing is wrong.

## Idea

The game in one line, with its pillars. Gate: does the line excite?

## Gathering

Braindumps, a rant room, brainstorming. Gathering never closes: ideas keep coming, and land as tickets for later. Gate: the director calls the outline.

## Outline

A basic outline of a plan, made from the ideas. Gate: does it say what to prototype?

## Prototype

Trying things: a greybox, the outline and the size of the map, features. A prototype is thrown away. Gate: is the core loop fun in a greybox? ([Loop First](game-design-laws-experience.md#loop-first))

## How To Apply

- Concept is a pile of gathering, and unorganized by nature. Its material is parsed from the director's prompts and brainstorms.
- The phases of Concept may overlap and loop back.
- Concept has no tickets of its own. An idea that Gathering files for later is the one ticket it leaves.
