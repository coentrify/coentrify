# Measure Sound, Never Guess

The level of a new sound is measured, never guessed.

## Why

The agent cannot listen.

## How to Apply

- Read the loudness against a sound the director already knows: another scene's bed, a click.
- A level worked out on paper misleads, since filtered soft noise has little energy up high.
- A reading counts only when the mix was silent before it: a long recording bleeds into the next reading.
- Silence the scene's own random sounds and beds first, and wait for silence between readings.

## In A Browser

- A throwaway probe spec taps an analyzer onto everything reaching the speakers.
- The reel's track is checked per half second.
