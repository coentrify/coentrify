# Game Flavors: Era

When the world is set, and how true it stays to its time. A world has one era. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Period Faithful

The world is set in one decade, and stays true to it: its objects, its words and its limits.

Example: a story in the 1920s, where news travels by telegram.

Conflicts: Mixed Era, Retro Future, Present Day and Timeless.

## Mixed Era

The world mixes decades on purpose, and the world gives the reason.

Example: a city rebuilt from salvage, where every decade's leftovers stand side by side.

Conflicts: Period Faithful, Retro Future, Present Day and Timeless.

## Retro Future

The future as an older decade imagined it.

Example: chrome rockets and ray guns, the year 2000 as the fifties pictured it.

Conflicts: Period Faithful, Mixed Era, Present Day and Timeless.

## Present Day

The world as it is now.

Example: a city as it is today, phones and all.

Conflicts: Period Faithful, Mixed Era, Retro Future and Timeless.

## Timeless

A world of its own, with no real decade behind it.

Example: a kingdom with a calendar of its own.

Conflicts: Period Faithful, Mixed Era, Retro Future and Present Day.
