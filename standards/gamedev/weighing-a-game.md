# Weighing A Game

`coentrify weigh` reads a game's choices as a whole, and names what could bite once players have it: a cost nothing answers, choices that pull against each other, and choices left unmade. A finding is no verdict. The director decides, or the agent at `autonomous` ([Creative Agency](../autonomy/creative-agency.md)).

## Why

A cost looks small on its own layer. Beside the other picks it can double, clash or go unanswered, and the players find it first.

## What It Reads

- The picks in `docs/flavors.md`, gathered by code with what each decides and weighs: `node node_modules/coentrify/src/tools/scripts/roll-flavors.mjs weigh`.
- The game's laws doc, with the laws it leaves out and their reasons ([Game Design Laws](game-design-laws.md)).
- The audience profile, the patterns the game uses, its rules broken on purpose and its decided docs.

## What It Names

- A law a pick sets aside, which the laws doc still holds, or leaves out without a reason ([Game Flavors](game-flavors.md)).
- A cost on a pick's `Weighs:` line that nothing softens and no decision accepts, such as Skill Only in a game made for every type of player, with no Assist Options.
- Choices that pull against each other where no `Conflicts:` line says so, such as Juice at full strength in a Melancholic game.
- One cost taken on at several layers, so it doubles down, such as Permanent Death beside Costly mistakes and a Fixed difficulty set high.
- A choice left implicit: a layer the game needs with no pick, or a pick whose scope stays open.
- A pick the audience profile would rule out ([Audience Profile Template](audience-profile-template.md)).
- Rules broken on purpose that crowd the groove, or a break that does not resonate ([Rules Broken On Purpose](game-design-laws-own-style.md#rules-broken-on-purpose)).

## How To Apply

- Each finding names the picks or laws it comes from, what it may cost the player, and one way to soften it.
- The report names what holds up too: the picks that carry each other.
- A finding is accepted with its reason, softened, or answered with another pick. An accepted one is written beside the pick or the law it touches, so the next weigh names it as decided.
- The question on ratio, composition and synergy at the Game Design gate is answered with a weigh ([Gate Check Questions](gate-check-questions.md#design)).
- The game is weighed again before Release ([Gate Check Questions](gate-check-questions.md#live)), and whenever the director asks.
