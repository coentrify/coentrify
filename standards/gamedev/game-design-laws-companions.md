# Game Design Laws: Companions

Laws about the characters who stand with the player: companions, allies, associates and the people under or over the player. The index and the way a game uses them are in [Game Design Laws](game-design-laws.md).

## Characters Of Their Own

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

A companion or ally is a character of its own, never a voice for a part of the hero's ego. It has its own interesting conditions, orthogonal and complementary to the player's. The best have their own thing going on, and are available to go on quests with the player. Companions enrich the world: quest givers, associates to get value from and trade with, people to share the experience with, people under the player's management or above it, protectors, and people who widen the horizon.

Rests on: [Parasocial Bonds](../psychology/parasocial-bonds.md), [Presence](../psychology/presence.md).

## Bonds From Shared Circumstance

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

A companion relates to the player in a positive way, without ulterior motives: similar goals or a similar situation, such as both being victims of the same circumstance, a common enemy, or a need of each other. Shared experience bonds, and shared trauma or excitement bonds more. A rescue bonds either way: the companion saves the player, or the player rights an injustice done to them. Someone weak or helpless, like an abandoned puppy, bonds too.

Rests on: [Shared Experience](../psychology/shared-experience.md), [Similarity](../psychology/similarity.md), [Reciprocity](../psychology/reciprocity.md), [Baby Schema](../psychology/baby-schema.md).

## What The Player Cannot See

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

A companion reacts from its own experience, and never judges. It tells the player what the player cannot perceive, such as I heard that x happened, and never explains what is going on or what the player can already see ([Dialog Gives Context](game-design-laws-story.md#dialog-gives-context), [No Yellow Paint](game-design-laws-guidance.md#no-yellow-paint)).

Rests on: [Cognitive Load](../psychology/cognitive-load.md), [Rewards And Feedback](../psychology/rewards-and-feedback.md).

## Mirroring

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

A companion mirrors the player's advances. It opens up as the player opens up, and a romanceable companion flirts back. It may make the first move, then waits for the player: push and pull, the dynamics of a good relationship.

Rests on: [Reciprocity](../psychology/reciprocity.md).

## Only What Matters

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

A companion involves the player only in major things, never in menial ones.

Rests on: [Resuming An Interrupted Task](../psychology/resuming-an-interrupted-task.md), [Autonomy, Competence And Relatedness](../psychology/autonomy-competence-and-relatedness.md).

## A Loss Is Grieved

Relevant for: a game with companions or allies.

Losing a companion feels really bad, and the player grieves.

Rests on: [Parasocial Bonds](../psychology/parasocial-bonds.md).
