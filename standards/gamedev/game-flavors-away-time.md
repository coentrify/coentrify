# Game Flavors: Away Time

What the world does while the game is closed. [Game Flavors: Time](game-flavors-time.md) covers a game left running, and this layer covers the time between sessions. These are the four main ones, never all there are. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## How Away Time Mixes

- Away time mixes by scope: the mines catch up while the story waits: `- Away Time: Capped Catch-Up - the mines`, `- Away Time: Frozen World - the story`.
- A game whose world has no life between sessions leaves the layer out.

## Frozen World

Nothing happens while the game is closed, and the world waits for the player.

Example: in a bakery game the ovens stand cold until the player is back.

Weighs: no pull to come back, and no reward for it either.

## Capped Catch-Up

The world earns for the time away, up to a limit.

Example: a mining game pays out at most eight hours of ore on the return.

Weighs: a reason to come back and an economy kept in hand, and a cap reached can feel like a loss.

## Full Catch-Up

The world earns for every moment away, at its full rate.

Example: a garden grows every plant for the whole week the player was gone.

Weighs: a generous return, and a long absence can skip the pacing.

## World Moves On

The world goes on while the game is closed, for good and for ill. It sets [AFK Safe](game-design-laws-experience.md#afk-safe) aside, so a game that picks it leaves the law out with its reason.

Example: the village market sells out of bread, and weeds come up between the cabbages.

Weighs: a world that lives beside the player and a reason to visit, and an absence costs something, so its pull is weighed ([Compulsion Is Considered](game-design-laws-values.md#compulsion-is-considered)).

## Sources

- Public games, as references only: Stardew Valley for Frozen World, the offline earnings of idle games such as Cookie Clicker for the catch-ups, Animal Crossing for World Moves On.
