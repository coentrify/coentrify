# Game Flavors: Maker Presence

Whether the people who made the game show in it. [Personal Circle Names](game-flavors-naming.md#personal-circle-names) covers the people in the maker's life, and a single hidden nod is a rule broken on purpose ([Rules Broken On Purpose](game-design-laws-own-style.md#rules-broken-on-purpose)). The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Invisible Maker

The game never points at who made it.

Example: no name or face of the makers shows before the credits.

Weighs: the world stands on its own, and the game carries no personal touch.

Conflicts: Studio Nods and Developer Cameo.

## Studio Nods

The maker's other games and studio show up in the world as references.

Example: a poster in a shop window advertises the studio's earlier game.

Weighs: those who know get a reward, and the nod can pull others out of the world.

Conflicts: Invisible Maker.

## Developer Cameo

The makers appear in person, as characters.

Example: the makers sit at a corner table in the tavern, chatting about bugs.

Weighs: a warm personal touch, and a cameo can break the frame.

Conflicts: Invisible Maker.
