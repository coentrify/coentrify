# Game Flavors: The Secret

What the world's secret turns out to be, in a game built on a mystery. [Fair Surprise](game-design-laws-world.md#fair-surprise) sets how the twist lands, and the flavor sets what it is. These are the five main ones, never all there are. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## How Secrets Mix

- The flavors sit on two axes. Every Theory Is True, The Mundane Truth, One True Theory and Never Confirmed set how true the world's explanations are, and each rules out the others.
- Perspective Shift sets whose side the truth is on, and stands beside any of them but Never Confirmed.

## Every Theory Is True

Every wild explanation the world hints at is true, and the ones who sound crazy are right.

Example: the old man who swears the lighthouse talks to ships is right.

Conflicts: The Mundane Truth, One True Theory and Never Confirmed.

## The Mundane Truth

The uncanny has an ordinary cause: people.

Example: the haunting is the landlord, driving the tenants out.

Conflicts: Every Theory Is True, One True Theory and Never Confirmed.

## One True Theory

Most explanations are wrong, and the player sorts out the one that is right.

Example: three townsfolk each blame a different curse, and one of them is right.

Conflicts: Every Theory Is True, The Mundane Truth and Never Confirmed.

## Never Confirmed

The ambiguity holds to the end.

Example: the end leaves open whether the visitor was ever real.

Conflicts: Every Theory Is True, The Mundane Truth, One True Theory and Perspective Shift.

## Perspective Shift

The enemy turns out to be the wronged party, driven by a cause behind them, and the secret moves the player to see the conflict from their side. It pairs with [New Eyes](game-flavors-reveal.md#new-eyes), where knowing the secret turns how the player sees everything.

Example: the raiders who burn the border farms turn out to be exiles the council drove out, and the player has served the council all along.

Weighs: the conflict gains depth and the end lands on understanding, and it can sour the fights the player already won, so the game gives the player a way to end the conflict without beating the wronged.

Conflicts: Never Confirmed.
