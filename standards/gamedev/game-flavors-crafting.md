# Game Flavors: Crafting

How making things plays, and what each choice weighs, in a game where the player makes things. The flavors mix: a game picks one per product, such as its core product and its side items. What a mistake costs is its own layer ([Mistakes](game-flavors-mistakes.md)). The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Menu Crafting

A recipe runs from a menu, and the time goes to what the product is for. A game that picks it leaves out [Friction In The World](game-design-laws-world.md#friction-in-the-world), with its reason.

Example: a potion brews from a recipe list, and the time goes to the quest it is for.

Weighs: making stays quick, and the making itself is no play.

## Hands-On Physics

Every step is done by hand, with physics, and automation comes later as a reward ([Earned Progress](game-design-laws-progress.md#earned-progress)).

Example: every loaf is kneaded by hand, until the player can afford a mixer.

Weighs: the making is the play, and a step done the hundredth time can tire.

## Automation First

The player builds machines from the start, and the craft is the factory.

Example: conveyor belts from the first minute, and the craft is the line.

Weighs: the player designs systems, and loses the touch of making one thing.
