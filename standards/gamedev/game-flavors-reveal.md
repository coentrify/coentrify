# Game Flavors: Reveal

How a game's secret unfolds over its length, in a game that has one. The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Shifting World

The player discovers the secret gradually, and the world's mood shifts with each step, toward a late twist.

Example: each clue shifts the music and the weather a little, until the last one turns the story.

Conflicts: Late Twist, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, New Eyes, Quiet Realization and Nested Secrets.

## Late Twist

The world stays the same until a single reveal turns it.

Example: the friendly mayor turns out to be the villain in the last chapter.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, New Eyes, Quiet Realization and Nested Secrets.

## Known From The Start

The player knows the secret, and the characters do not.

Example: the player knows the house is haunted, and the new family does not.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Late Twist, Slow Burn, New Eyes, Quiet Realization and Nested Secrets.

## Slow Burn

Clues pile up, with no single twist.

Example: letters found one by one add up to the truth, with no single moment that tells it.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Late Twist, Known From The Start, New Eyes, Quiet Realization and Nested Secrets.

## New Eyes

The world stays the same, and knowing the secret turns how the player sees all of it. The contrast between what the player knows and what the characters still pretend is what hits.

Example: the player finds out the secret everyone has kept from the start, and they go on pretending.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Late Twist, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, Quiet Realization and Nested Secrets.

## Quiet Realization

The world stays the same, and what the player comes to see gently shifts what matters.

Example: so busy helping the people around them that they never spend time with them, at the end the player lets progress go and is happy just being with their companions.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Late Twist, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, New Eyes and Nested Secrets.

## Nested Secrets

A secret the player learns early, often while the characters stay unaware, hides a deeper one that turns the story late. The Secret holds the deeper one, since it turns the story.

Example: the player learns in the first hour that the orphanage's founder is not who she claims, and only at the end who she was.

Weighs: two payoffs, an early hook and a late turn, and the first can make the second feel expected.

Conflicts: Shifting World, Late Twist, Known From The Start, Slow Burn, New Eyes and Quiet Realization.
