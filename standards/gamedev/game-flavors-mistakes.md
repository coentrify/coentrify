# Game Flavors: Mistakes

What a mistake costs the player, in making, building or anything else the player can get wrong. Whatever the flavor, a mistake never makes the game unwinnable ([No Softlock](game-design-laws-progress.md#no-softlock)). The index and the way a game uses its flavors are in [Game Flavors](game-flavors.md).

## Forgiving

A mistake is undone: an undo, a retry, and a lost item comes back.

Example: a vase knocked off the table reappears on its shelf at dawn.

Weighs: the player experiments freely, and a step loses its tension.

Conflicts: Costly.

## Costly

A mistake costs material or time, and failing is part of the play.

Example: a cake left too long in the oven is lost, and so are its eggs.

Weighs: failing means something, and a costly mistake frustrates.

Conflicts: Forgiving.
