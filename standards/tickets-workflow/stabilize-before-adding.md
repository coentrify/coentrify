# Stabilize Before Adding

While a version is being made stable and clean, its plan holds bugs and tweaks to what exists only. Anything new, a feature, a look, a preset, goes into its epic and phase, off the plan, even when the director mentions it in the middle of a playtest or a user test. The plan says so in its focus line.

## Why

Every version reaches stable and clean.

## How To Apply

- Stable and clean comes first, then the short-term roadmap, then new things.
- New things ride in from playtest and user test notes easily, and a version that mixes them with its fixes is never stable.
- When parsing a note, ask whether the thing exists in the product today. If not, it is new and goes into its epic, unless the director explicitly pulls it into the version.
