# Processing The Ideas

Raw ideas gather in a dump in `docs/ideas/`, sorted by the phase that will use them, and leave it topic by topic once their phase is next, each into a decided doc.

## Why

A pile of a hundred ideas stays findable, and nothing fixes a phase or a design before the work in front of it has checked out.

## The Ideas Folder

- `docs/ideas/dump.md` holds the raw ideas, written from the director's prompts and brainstorms. The request log keeps the words, and the dump keeps the ideas ([Request Log](../records/request-log.md)).
- A page per category is pulled from the dump to find things by, such as characters, quests, places and vehicles in a game. Each line holds a name, one line of description and the entries it comes from.
- A category page decides nothing, so it is no refinement ahead of its phase.

## How To Apply

- Sort: every entry gets the phase that will use it. An entry with several destinations is processed in parts.
- A detail within a topic, such as a single side character, a side quest, a reference or a name in a game, is Content. It waits in the dump until its content is made ([Content Template](../gamedev/content-template.md)).
- What needs no later phase is processed at once: a law, a rule about the process, a choice about the launch.
- Everything else waits until its phase is next, or starts earlier where its phase allows it, as a game's lore does ([Work Tracking](work-tracking.md#phases)). Then it is processed per topic, never per entry, only as deep as the phase's gate needs: every entry of the topic that the gate needs is read, what stands now is kept, and a conflict goes to the director.
- Before a topic's ticket is refined, each entry it would read is sorted: what its phase decides, or Content. Its acceptance criteria name only the first.
- A topic is processed under a ticket of its phase, such as a game's Lore or Game ticket, and ends in a decided doc with the director's verdict.
- A processed part leaves the dump and its category pages. The dump shrinks toward empty, and new ideas keep coming in.
